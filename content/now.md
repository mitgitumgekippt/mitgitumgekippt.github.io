## What is a /now page?

A `/now` page is about what someone is currently focused on and working toward. Think of it like what you'd say to a friend you haven’t seen in a year — a snapshot of what matters most right now.

Unlike social media updates or blog posts, a `/now` page focuses on the bigger picture rather than day-to-day highlights. It also contrasts with an `/about` page, which is more static and background-focused. If you want to learn more, check out [nownownow.com/about](https://nownownow.com/about).
