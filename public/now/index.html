What is a /now page?#

The ‘/now’-page is about what a person is focused on and working on at the moment. Imagine, what you would try to say to friend, which you haven’t seen for a year. In difference to social media updates or blog posts, the ‘/now’-page is more focused on the big picture instead of selected recent events. It also stays in contrast to an ‘/about’-page, which is a general introduction and the background of a person. If you want to know more about this, check out nownownow.com/about.
